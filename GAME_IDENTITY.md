# Scale Shift / קנה מידה

**Core verbs:** walk, inspect, select, uniformly resize, rotate, test, commit, cross, undo.

**First minute:** approach an open six-metre void and its far sunlight; select the tall arch and enlarge to two. Its width and height intersect the receiving aperture. Select the low gallery, reason 6 / 4 = 3 / 2, enlarge uniformly, commit the physical structure and walk through its 2.1 m opening. No answer gate or teleport replaces this crossing.

**Decision and changing state:** choose a proportional family, rational factor in 1/20 steps, and a supported orientation. All three outer dimensions and inner headroom change together. Committed transforms create rendered and walkable surfaces from the same measured state. Five continuous chambers introduce inverse scale, impossible aspect ratio/orientation, a calibrated drawing and an unhinted two-span transfer.

**Failure/recovery:** conflicting surface/dimension remains visible; rejection keeps the last valid geometry. No edits while occupied. Falls return to the current entrance. Undo removes both mesh and support; pause/retry are always reachable. No lives, timers, jumps or collection economy.

**Win:** physically enter the final light well after crossing all six spans. Earlier museum spaces remain behind the player, open to revisiting.

**Camera and materials:** first-person eye height 1.65 m, actual perspective and occlusion, monumental ivory concrete, sharp sunlight, long shadows, ultramarine interventions. A Chrome-Tripo light-well sculpture is decorative, never a collider. Frame/deck dimensions stay procedural and explicit.

**Motion/sound:** preview scales with physical depth; structural resonance marks a commit, footfalls reinforce walking, quiet dust/light provides atmosphere. Respect reduced motion and mute.

**Portfolio comparison:** Scale Shift's governing decision is simultaneous dimensional fit and its reward is a discovered walkable route. It shares neither Percent Pulse's attacks, Hypotenuse Rush's grappling/momentum, Ringwarden's material allocation nor Equal & Opposite's algebra cards. No robot, courier, retrieval, combat, mission board or toy diorama.

Visual thesis: quiet monumental concrete carved by sunlight, with precise ultramarine geometry that becomes architecture.
Content plan: immediate world view, one entrance action, minimal active measurements, optional controls/help, final light well.
Interaction thesis: restrained entrance fade; continuous first-person parallax; preview-to-solid structural settling.
