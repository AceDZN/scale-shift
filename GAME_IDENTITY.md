# Scale Shift / קנה מידה

Current identity: the guided rebuild approved after the owner's feedback about confusing controls and flow. The original five-room design is historical context in `docs/BUILD_HANDOFF.md` and Git.

**Core verbs:** drag, release, connect, walk, discover, undo.

**Opening:** start in the museum facing a six-metre gap and one blue handle. Drag the gallery; its length, width and height change together. Overshooting marks the actual overlap and leaves the handle usable. At a valid transform the prompt says to release. Releasing turns the preview into solid architecture, then a footprint marker invites one click to walk across it. No title screen, pointer lock, movement keys or separate commit mode is required.

**Decision and changing state:** choose a uniform factor in 1/20 steps between 1/2 and 3. The first gallery grows from 4 m to 6 m at 3/2. The twelve-step staircase shrinks from a 4 m run and 2 m rise to a 2 m run and 1 m rise at 1/2. The raised shore then requires 4 m → 3 m at 3/4, followed by 3 m → 6 m at 2. Four interactions span three spaces with different directions and elevation. Family selection, rotation and drawing-scale puzzles are outside this rebuilt slice.

**Failure and recovery:** a conflicting surface receives local hatching and plain Hebrew feedback. Invalid release retains any previous valid geometry. Escape cancels a drag. Undo removes the current unoccupied connection, and pause/retry remain available. A second click while walking stops safely; resuming follows the remaining supported route. There are no lives or timers.

**Win:** physically traverse all four committed structures and reach the light court. One persistent 3D world retains the five platforms and earlier structures. A preview cannot support walking or advance progress.

**Camera and materials:** first-person eye height 1.65 m, actual perspective and occlusion, ivory concrete, sunlight, ultramarine manipulation and water. Each arrival frames the next structure obliquely to reveal growth in all dimensions. Climbing changes the physical eye elevation; the final walk turns toward the original Chrome-Tripo sculpture. That decorative GLB never supplies measured geometry or support.

**Motion and sound:** the complete preview scales uniformly. Valid release triggers structural resonance and a short material wash; geometry stays fixed. One destination click starts continuous walking with parallax and footsteps. Reduced motion disables decorative pulses, water motion and the wash; essential walking has no head bob. Mute is always available. Touch uses the same gesture with reachable drag sensitivity and portrait framing.

**Portfolio distinction:** uniform dimensional fit creates a route through architecture. The governing interaction remains spatial scaling and traversal; there is no combat, grappling, collection, delivery or card economy.

This is a short playable slice. Numerical tests, ordinary mouse/touch input and actual Chrome checks verify its mechanics. Human clarity, enjoyment and learning gains remain unmeasured.
